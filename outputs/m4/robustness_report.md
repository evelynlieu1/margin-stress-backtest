## Limitations

- Variants change one setting at a time; combinations are not tried.
- 'without 2020' leaves 2020 out of scoring only; the margins still learn from 2020 data.
- The AUD/USD sub-periods have about 380 and 2,100 test periods: C2 has little power in 2007-2009.
- WTI's R1 is a fixed dollar amount from before 2007, when oil was much cheaper, so it under-covers by design.
